Two teams of 5-6 have one representative stand at the front where they each have a buzzer. The host reads out a question which 100 people have responded to. The responses have been aggregated to 5-10 answers with frequencies on the screen. The answers are hidden at first. The first representative to buzz gets to guess an answer, after which the other representative gets to guess.
Whoever gets the answer with a higher frequency gets the option to choose which team will try to guess the rest of the answers. The chosen team will have up to three strikes for incorrect answers. If they fail, then the other team can steal all the points by getting one more correct answer.

We’ll use questions from the real show to avoid polling ourselves. We can build a website that runs locally and has open page for display and one for hosts. The display page will show the question and hidden answers plus team names and points. The hosts page will show all answers and make them clickable. If the contestant is correct, the host will click the answer, and the display page will make a ding noise and show the answer with an animation similar to the show. Once, the last answer is revealed, the winning music will start playing. If the contestant is wrong, there will be an “incorrect” noise along with an X appearing on the screen for a few moments. The purpose of this is to only use one laptop to run the whole game.
